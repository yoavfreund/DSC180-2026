# Overview

Welcome to the capstone program! The capstone program is a two-quarter sequence (Fall 2026 and Winter 2027) in which you will be mentored by a faculty member or industry expert in their domain of expertise. By the end of Quarter 2, you will design and execute a project from that domain in teams. You can see the projects from last year at [dsc-capstone.org/showcase-26](https://dsc-capstone.org/showcase-26).

At a high level, here’s how the capstone program is organized:

- **Quarter 1 (DSC 180A):** You gain background information in your mentor’s domain by replicating a known result. By the end of Quarter 1, you will have completed a replication project (the “Quarter 1 Project”) and will have a proposal for a more independent project (the “Quarter 2 Project”, or the capstone project).
- **Quarter 2 (DSC 180B):** You execute the Quarter 2 Project you proposed at the end of Quarter 1.

For Domain D31 (*Ensemble of Agents*), that path means: in Quarter 1 you learn ensemble methods (bagging, AdaBoost) and reimplement them as communicating hybrid agents; in Quarter 2 you build on those agent interfaces to create a general-purpose system whose coordinator chooses methods, combinations, and how to measure and optimize performance. See [curriculum.md](curriculum.md) for the full plan.
